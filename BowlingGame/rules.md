Bowling Game Rules:
1. Game Structure: 10 frames, 2 rolls per frame (except last frame)

2. A strike: when all ten pins are nocked down with first roll

3. A spare: when all ten pins are nocked down with two rolls

4. Each pin knocked down is 1 point unless there has been a spare or strike, where a spare would also add the points from the next roll and a strike would add the next two

5. Last Frame: Can have 3 rolls if strike or spare

6. Perfect Game: 12 strikes (300 points)
